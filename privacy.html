Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

The short version
  • Your snips are stored in the app on your iPhone. There is no snipcam account and no public feed.
  • When your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, snipcam backs up your snips to your own private iCloud, run by Apple. We cannot see them.
  • AutoSnip cuts videos from Photos on your iPhone. Your originals are never edited or deleted.
  • We collect limited usage analytics to improve the app. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we are

snipcam is a personal video app for iPhone made by Eric Nguyen (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the snipcam app and this website. Contact us at ericnguyendsg@gmail.com.

Your snips and your storage

Recordings, audio, thumbnails, dates, favourites, captions, stacks, collections and preferences are stored in the app’s storage on your iPhone. We do not receive them. snipcam has no public social feed and does not require an account.

iCloud backup

When your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, snipcam backs up your library automatically to your private CloudKit database, hosted by Apple under your Apple Account and using your iCloud storage. The backup holds your snips and their details (dates, favourites, likes, notes, stamps and any saved location labels), your stacks and their order, and each day’s title and cover. If you lock a stack with a passcode, a scrambled form of the passcode is stored in encrypted fields so the lock carries over to your other devices. We cannot access your private CloudKit database. To stop the backup, turn snipcam off in iPhone Settings under your name, iCloud, Saved to iCloud. Turning it off does not by itself delete copies already in iCloud. Apple also manages any device backups you have turned on.

Camera, microphone and Photos

Camera and microphone access let you capture snips with sound. Photos access lets you import photos and videos, create AutoSnips and save exports to your library. You can change these permissions at any time in iPhone Settings.

Locked stacks and Face ID

You can lock a stack. snipcam asks iOS to confirm Face ID or your passcode before opening it. iOS tells snipcam only whether the check passed. We never receive your face data.

AutoSnip

AutoSnip looks through videos in Photos from the dates you choose, picks a few seconds from each, and saves those short cuts as a collection in the app. This includes the AutoSnip you can make while setting up snipcam, which becomes your first collection. The analysis and cutting happen on your iPhone. Your originals in Photos are not edited or deleted. If a video is stored only in iCloud Photos, iOS may download it so it can be cut.

Search, looks and other features on your iPhone

To let you search your snips (for example, for “beach” or “haircut”), snipcam uses Apple’s on-device image analysis to note what appears in each snip, along with your captions and stack names. Film looks, poster frames and AutoSnip cuts are also made on your iPhone. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Sharing into snipcam

When you share a photo, video or Live Photo to snipcam from another app, it is copied into snipcam on your iPhone. The original stays where it was.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, snipcam schedules a weekly reminder on your iPhone. It is not sent through our servers. You can turn it off in iPhone Settings.

Location

If you allow it, snipcam reads your location while you use the app. It pins each snip to your snip map, and it can add the place name to the snip's timestamp. The location and place name stay with the snip on your iPhone and in your private iCloud backup. They are never sent to us and never used for advertising. You can turn location off at any time in iPhone Settings. Capturing and playing snips work without it.

Separately, our analytics service estimates an approximate location, such as country and city, from the IP address of each event, as described under Usage analytics.

Usage analytics and diagnostics

snipcam uses PostHog for product analytics. Events describe app use, such as opening the app, using a feature, completing a purchase or export, and performance or error diagnostics. Purchase events include which snipcam Pro plan was bought or restored, never payment details. PostHog processes a randomly generated installation identifier, device and app details, and your IP address, from which it estimates an approximate location such as country and city. The service is located in the United States. Our analytics never include your snips, captions, file names or saved location labels. Session replay and automatic screen capture are turned off. We do not use analytics to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Purchases

Apple processes snipcam Pro purchases and subscriptions. snipcam uses StoreKit transaction information to unlock Pro and restore purchases. We never receive your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Feedback form and beta testing

The form on this website sends your message, and your email address if you choose to add it, to our own form service, hosted on Vercel, where it is stored privately. We use it only to read your idea or feedback and to reply if you left an address. To have a message removed, email us.

If you join the waitlist, your email address is stored privately in the same service, with the date and the country your connection came from. We use it only to tell you when snipcam is available, and we delete it on request.

The beta is distributed through Apple’s TestFlight. Apple shares with us the crash reports and feedback you choose to send from TestFlight, along with the email address of your Apple Account if you include it.

Sharing, retention and deletion

Snips leave the app only when you share or export them, or when they are backed up to your own iCloud. Shared copies follow the rules of wherever you send them. Deleted snips stay in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days unless you delete them permanently sooner. iCloud copies are deleted once your device syncs, so a device that is offline may keep copies until it reconnects. Exports and device backups are managed separately. Before deleting the app, save any snips you want to keep.

We keep support emails as long as needed to answer you and follow up. Analytics data is kept according to our PostHog project’s retention settings. We do not sell your personal data.

This website

This website counts visits with PostHog, the same analytics service the app uses. It records which page was opened, how long it stayed open, the referring site, browser and device type, and clicks on the TestFlight link. PostHog estimates an approximate location, such as country and city, from your IP address. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, so visits are not linked to each other or to you. We use no advertising cookies or trackers. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process standard access logs to deliver and secure the site.

Children

snipcam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete information we hold about you by emailing us. Snips stored only on your iPhone are managed in the app, and you can delete them there at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
