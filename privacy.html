Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

The short version
  • Your snips are stored in the app on your iPhone. There is no snipcam account and no public feed.
  • iCloud sync, if you turn it on, stores copies in your own private iCloud, run by Apple.
  • AutoSnip cuts videos from Photos on your iPhone. Your originals are never edited or deleted.
  • We collect limited usage analytics to improve the app. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we are

snipcam is a personal video app for iPhone made by Eric Nguyen (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the snipcam app and this website. Contact us at ericnguyendsg@gmail.com.

Your snips and your storage

Recordings, audio, thumbnails, dates, favourites, captions, stacks, collections and preferences are stored in the app’s storage on your iPhone. We do not receive them. snipcam has no public social feed and does not require an account.

iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, snipcam sends your snips and their details (dates, favourites, notes and any saved location labels) to your private CloudKit database, hosted by Apple under your Apple Account and using your iCloud storage. We cannot access your private CloudKit database. Turning sync off does not by itself delete copies already in iCloud. Apple also manages any device backups you have turned on.

Camera, microphone and Photos

Camera and microphone access let you capture snips with sound. Photos access lets you import photos and videos, create AutoSnips and save exports to your library. You can change these permissions at any time in iPhone Settings.

AutoSnip

AutoSnip looks through videos in Photos from the dates you choose, picks a few seconds from each, and saves those short cuts as a collection in the app. This includes the AutoSnip you can make while setting up snipcam, which becomes your first collection. The analysis and cutting happen on your iPhone. Your originals in Photos are not edited or deleted. If a video is stored only in iCloud Photos, iOS may download it so it can be cut.

Search, looks and other features on your iPhone

To let you search your snips (for example, for “beach” or “haircut”), snipcam uses Apple’s on-device image analysis to note what appears in each snip, along with your captions and stack names. Film looks, poster frames and AutoSnip cuts are also made on your iPhone. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Sharing into snipcam

When you share a photo, video or Live Photo to snipcam from another app, it is copied into snipcam on your iPhone. The original stays where it was.

Notifications

If you turn on Weekly rewind, snipcam schedules a reminder on your iPhone. It is not sent through our servers. You can turn it off in snipcam’s Settings or in iPhone Settings.

Location

snipcam does not collect your location. Snips made with an early version of the app may carry an approximate place name; it stays with that snip and its iCloud copy, and is never used for advertising.

Usage analytics and diagnostics

snipcam uses PostHog for product analytics. Events describe app use, such as opening the app, using a feature, completing a purchase or export, and performance or error diagnostics. PostHog may process a randomly generated installation identifier, device and app details, and network information such as your IP address. The service is located in the United States. Our analytics never include your snips, captions, file names or saved location labels. Session replay and automatic screen capture are turned off. We do not use analytics to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Purchases

Apple processes snipcam Pro purchases and subscriptions. snipcam uses StoreKit transaction information to unlock Pro and restore purchases. We never receive your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Launch emails

If you leave your email address on this website, we use it only to tell you when snipcam launches on the App Store, and then remove it from the list. If the website opens your mail app instead, the message comes to our support inbox and is handled the same way. We do not share the list or use it for anything else. To be removed sooner, email us.

Sharing, retention and deletion

Snips leave the app only when you share or export them, or turn on iCloud sync. Shared copies follow the rules of wherever you send them. Deleted snips stay in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days unless you delete them permanently sooner. iCloud copies are deleted once your device syncs, so a device that is offline may keep copies until it reconnects. Exports and device backups are managed separately. Before deleting the app, save any snips you want to keep.

We keep support emails as long as needed to answer you and follow up. Analytics data is kept according to our PostHog project’s retention settings. We do not sell your personal data.

This website

This website uses no analytics, advertising cookies or trackers. Our hosting provider may process standard access logs to deliver and secure the site.

Children

snipcam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete information we hold about you by emailing us. Snips stored only on your iPhone are managed in the app, and you can delete them there at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
